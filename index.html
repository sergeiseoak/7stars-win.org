How Betting Odds Work

Sportsbook odds indicate the potential return associated with a particular outcome. Decimal odds include the original stake when calculating the total potential payout.

For example, decimal odds of 2.50 on a hypothetical BDT 100 stake would represent:

Original stake: BDT 100

Potential total return: BDT 250

Potential profit: BDT 150

This is a mathematical example rather than an actual 7StarsWin offer or betting recommendation.

Odds may change before a match or during live events. They also reflect the bookmaker's pricing margin and should not be interpreted as a guarantee of any outcome.

Is 7StarsWin Legal in Bangladesh?

Online gambling and sports betting are prohibited in Bangladesh under the Gambling Prevention Act, 2026 (Act No. 98 of 2026).

The law took effect on 1 July 2026 and replaced the Public Gambling Act, 1867. It addresses both conventional gambling and activities conducted through digital platforms.

Its provisions cover online sports betting, remote gambling, virtual casinos and certain financial transactions connected with gambling.

Penalties for Online Betting

Section 17 of the Gambling Prevention Act, 2026, establishes penalties for online betting offences.

A person convicted under the relevant provisions may face:

Imprisonment of up to seven years.

A fine of up to BDT 50 million (Tk 5 crore).

Both imprisonment and a fine.

Other provisions address gambling-related financial transfers, digital payment systems and activities involving gambling software or platforms.

These penalties are statutory maximums; the consequences of an individual case depend on the offence and applicable legal proceedings.

What Is an Android APK?

An APK is the installation package used for Android applications. It allows software to be installed on a compatible device.

Warning Signs of an Unsafe Application

Suspicious Android download pages may display several warning signs:

Mobile Browser vs Dedicated App

A mobile website opens through a browser such as Chrome and generally does not require an installation file.
