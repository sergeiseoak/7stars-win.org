Is 7StarsWin Registration Legal in Bangladesh?

Bangladesh introduced the Gambling Prevention Act, 2026 (Act No. 98 of 2026), effective from 1 July 2026. The legislation replaced the Public Gambling Act, 1867 and introduced provisions covering online betting, digital gambling platforms and associated financial activities.

Online betting is prohibited under the legislation. Section 17 provides for imprisonment of up to seven years, a fine of up to BDT 50 million, or both, for offences covered by the relevant online betting provisions.

The law does not establish a blanket rule that every ordinary act of creating an online account is itself a gambling offence. Whether particular conduct violates the legislation depends on the activity and applicable provisions.

Nevertheless, residents should not assume that registration or betting through a foreign website is permitted simply because the site accepts Bangladeshi contact information.

For the legislation itself, consult the Bangladesh Laws database. Our Online Betting Safety in Bangladesh page examines the broader legal and financial context.

If identity documents were uploaded: Keep a record of the website and communications involved. Consider reporting suspected identity misuse to the relevant authorities.
